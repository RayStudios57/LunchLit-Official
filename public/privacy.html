Privacy Policy for LunchLIT

Last updated: October 17, 2026

LunchLIT ("we", "our", or "us") operates the LunchLIT mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

Types of Data Collected:

Account & Profile Information: If you create an account, we collect user credentials (such as email and authentication data managed securely via Supabase) to sync your schedule, tasks, and notes across devices.
Academic & Productivity Data: Information you input voluntarily, such as class schedules, assignments, study timers, and brag sheet entries.
Device & Usage Data: Basic diagnostic data and app performance metrics to ensure stability.

2. Use of Data

LunchLIT uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the LunchLIT service.
  • To allow you to participate in interactive features of our app.
  • To provide customer support and improve our student productivity tools.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

LunchLIT utilizes secure third-party backend services (such as Supabase for database management and authentication). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or sensitive user data to any third parties.

4. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. We use modern cryptographic protocols (such as HTTPS encryption in transit) to protect data sent to and from our servers. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Data Protection Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Access & Correction: You can update your profile and schedule details directly within the app.
  • Account Deletion: If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so directly from within the app settings, or by visiting our account deletion web resource. Upon request, all associated user data will be permanently deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

LunchLIT is designed as a student productivity tool for high school students. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
